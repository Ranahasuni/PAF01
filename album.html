ARIRANG

BTS’s Arirang (2026) marks the group’s highly anticipated comeback after a long hiatus, blending global pop with deep Korean heritage. Named after the iconic traditional folk song Arirang, the album reflects themes of longing, reunion, and perseverance, connecting BTS’s personal growth with shared cultural identity. Featuring 14 tracks across multiple physical editions, the release has already broken pre-order records and set the stage for the massive Arirang World Tour, showcasing BTS’s unmatched global influence. More than just music, Arirangsymbolizes reunion, loyalty, and emotional resonance between BTS and their fans, ARMY, making it a milestone in both their career and K-pop history.
